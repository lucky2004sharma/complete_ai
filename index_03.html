    04 // ENGINE GUIDE

    What happens to your photo

    The page is the control surface; prototype.py performs the real decode, resize, mode fit, enhancement, encoding, and verification.

    01

    Analyze the real source

    The engine reads the file bytes, detects format, corrects EXIF orientation, reports dimensions, and finds the closest common aspect ratio.

    02

    Fit without stretching

    Cover fills the full canvas with a centered crop. Contain preserves the entire photo and uses transparent or white padding when needed.

    03

    Export and verify

    The selected quality tier controls pixel multiplier and encoding strength. Upscaled results receive mild sharpening before the output is verified.
